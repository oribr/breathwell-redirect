# Privacy Policy **Last Updated:** January 13, 2025 **Version:** 1.0 ## About BreathWell BreathWell is operated by an independent developer. This app provides breathing exercises and wellness tracking tools to help you manage stress and improve mental well-being. ## Information We Collect ### Information You Provide - **Account Information:** Email address, name (optional), age (optional), gender (optional) - **Wellness Data:** Mood logs, breathing session data, journal entries - **Usage Data:** How you interact with the app's features ### Automatically Collected - **Device Information:** Device type, operating system version - **Analytics:** App usage patterns, crash reports ## How We Use Your Information We use your information to: - Provide and improve the app's features - Generate personalized AI wellness reports - Create voice-guided meditation sessions - Sync your data across devices - Send important updates about the app ## AI-Powered Features BreathWell uses OpenAI's services to generate: - Personalized wellness reports analyzing your mood patterns - Voice-guided meditation scripts with text-to-speech When you use these features, your wellness data is sent to OpenAI's servers for processing. OpenAI may use this data according to their own privacy policy. We recommend reviewing [OpenAI's Privacy Policy](https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy). **Important:** We do NOT share your personally identifiable information (name, email) with AI providers. Only anonymized wellness data is used for AI generation. ## Data Storage Your data is stored securely using: - **Supabase:** Cloud database for account and wellness data - **Encryption:** All data is encrypted in transit and at rest - **Location:** Servers are located in the United States ## Data Sharing We do NOT sell your personal information. We only share data with: 1. **Service Providers:** - Supabase (database hosting) - OpenAI (AI report and voice generation) - Apple/Google (payment processing through App Store/Play Store) 2. **Legal Requirements:** If required by law or to protect rights and safety ## Your Rights You have the right to: - **Access** your data at any time - **Export** all your data - **Delete** your account and all associated data - **Opt-out** of AI features (you can use breathing exercises without AI) To exercise these rights, use the settings in the app or contact: [YOUR EMAIL] ## Children's Privacy BreathWell is NOT intended for users under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has provided data, please contact us. ## Data Retention - **Active Accounts:** Data stored as long as account is active - **Deleted Accounts:** All data permanently deleted within 30 days - **Backups:** May remain in encrypted backups for up to 90 days ## Security We implement industry-standard security measures: - Encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS) - Encryption at rest - Secure authentication (email verification required) - Regular security updates However, no method is 100% secure. Use strong passwords and keep your device secure. ## International Users If you use BreathWell outside the United States, your data may be transferred to and stored in the US. By using the app, you consent to this transfer. ## Changes to Privacy Policy We may update this policy.
