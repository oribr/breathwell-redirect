Frequently Asked Questions

How do I cancel my subscription?

To cancel your subscription:

  • iOS: Settings → Your Name → Subscriptions → BreathWell → Cancel
  • Android: Play Store → Menu → Subscriptions → BreathWell → Cancel

How do I delete my account?

Open the BreathWell app, go to Settings → Account → Delete Account. All your data will be permanently deleted within 30 days.

How do I export my data?

In the app: Settings → Privacy → Export Data. You'll receive a JSON file with all your wellness data.

Is my data secure?

Yes! We use industry-standard encryption and secure cloud storage. Read our Privacy Policy for details.

What if I experience issues with breathing exercises?

Stop immediately if you feel dizzy or uncomfortable. Consult a healthcare professional if you have any medical concerns. BreathWell is a wellness tool, not medical advice.
